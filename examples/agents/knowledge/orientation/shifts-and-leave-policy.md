# Shifts, swaps and leave (policy, fictional)

- Rotas are published six weeks ahead in the e-Roster app.
- Ask for a shift swap in the app at least two weeks ahead; the ward manager approves it.
- No more than three long days in a row, and at least eleven hours between the end of one shift and the start of the next.
- If you are sick, phone the nurse in charge on the ward before your shift starts, then record it in the e-Roster app.
- Annual leave is booked in the app; at most two nurses from one ward can be on leave in the same week.
