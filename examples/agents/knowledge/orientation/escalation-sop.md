# Escalating a deteriorating patient (standard operating procedure, fictional)

Every patient's observations are scored with NEWS2.

- Score 1 to 4: tell the nurse in charge, who decides how often to repeat the observations.
- Score 5 or 6, or 3 in any single parameter: tell the nurse in charge straight away and call the critical care outreach team on extension 4455. Repeat observations at least hourly.
- Score 7 or more: call the medical emergency team on 2222 and stay with the patient.

Write down every escalation in the patient's notes: who you told, when, and what was decided. If you are worried about a patient whatever the score, escalate. You will never be criticised for escalating.
