# Medicines on the ward (standard operating procedure, fictional)

- Give a medicine only when it is prescribed on the electronic prescribing system for that patient, at that dose, by that route, at that time.
- Controlled drugs need two registered nurses to check, give and sign.
- New nurses give medicines under their preceptor's supervision until their medicines competency is signed off, usually in week three.
- If a dose looks wrong, or you are unsure, do not give it: ask the prescriber or the ward pharmacist (bleep 312).
- Report every medicine error or near miss on the incident system the same day.
