# Ward 7 handbook (Riverside General Hospital, fictional)

Ward 7 is a 28-bed acute medical ward on the second floor of the Lister Building. Most patients are admitted through the emergency department with chest infections, heart failure, diabetes problems or falls.

## Shifts
- Early: 07:00 to 15:00
- Late: 13:00 to 21:00
- Long day: 07:00 to 19:30
- Night: 19:15 to 07:30

Handover is at 07:00, 13:00 and 19:15 at the nurses' station, using the SBAR handover sheet. Arrive ten minutes early for your first handover.

## Your first two weeks
New nurses work supernumerary for their first two weeks: you are not counted in the staffing numbers, and you work alongside your preceptor. You do not hold the drug keys or take charge of a bay on your own until your preceptor signs off your competencies.

## Who does what
The nurse in charge runs each shift and is your first contact for anything you are unsure about. The ward manager handles rotas, leave and welfare. The clinical educator runs teaching on Wednesday afternoons.
