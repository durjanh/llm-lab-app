# Hand hygiene and infection prevention (standard operating procedure, fictional)

Clean your hands at the five moments: before touching a patient, before a clean or aseptic procedure, after a risk of exposure to body fluids, after touching a patient, and after touching a patient's surroundings. Use soap and water, not gel, after caring for anyone with diarrhoea. Bare below the elbows in clinical areas: no wrist watches, no stoned rings, short sleeves.
