# First-week checklist for new nurses (fictional)

1. Collect your ID badge and smartcard from Security (ground floor, Lister Building) on day one.
2. Complete the e-learning modules: fire safety, information governance, safeguarding level 2 and basic life support.
3. Shadow your preceptor for at least four shifts.
4. Learn where the resuscitation trolley, the fire exits and the spill kit are on your ward.
5. Meet the clinical educator, who books your medicines competency assessment.
