# Brightwell Analytics: sales playbook

Brightwell Analytics is a fictional company, used for training. None of these details are real.

## What we sell

Brightwell Insight is a data platform for mid-sized organisations: it brings operational data from many systems into one place, with ready-made dashboards and a forecasting module. Typical customers replace spreadsheet reporting that takes days each month.

## Who we sell to (ideal customer)

- Industries: healthcare, retail, logistics and financial services.
- Size: 200 to 5,000 employees. Smaller organisations rarely need us; larger ones usually want bespoke work, which goes to the enterprise team.
- Region: the UK first, then the rest of Europe.
- Good signs: an approved budget for data work, hiring data roles, reporting still done in spreadsheets, a competitor contract up for renewal.

## Pricing (per month, billed annually)

| Organisation size | Price |
|---|---|
| Up to 250 employees | £1,500 |
| 251 to 2,000 employees | £4,000 |
| 2,001 to 5,000 employees | £7,500 |
| Over 5,000 employees | Bespoke: pass to the enterprise team |

Every plan includes onboarding and the forecasting module. There is a 30-day pilot for qualified leads.

## Discounts

- Account executives may offer up to 10% off for a three-year commitment or annual prepayment.
- Anything above 10% needs the sales director's written approval before it is mentioned to a customer. Never put a larger discount in writing without it.

## Qualifying a lead

Use BANT: budget, authority (are we talking to someone who can decide?), need, and timing. A lead is qualified when we know all four. Disqualify politely, with a reason recorded, when a lead is outside our ideal customer profile.

## Follow-up emails

- Short: four or five sentences. One clear next step, usually a 30-minute discovery call.
- Mention something specific to them (a signal from their research), never a generic pitch.
- No pressure tactics, no false urgency, no claims we cannot back up.
- Only email people who have agreed to hear from us, or who contacted us first. Never email anyone who has opted out. At most one follow-up per person in any seven days.
- The opt-out line is added automatically.
