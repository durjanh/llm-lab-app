# Harbourside Medical Centre: prescriptions, test results and urgent care

Harbourside Medical Centre is a fictional GP practice, used for training. None of these details are real.

## Repeat prescriptions

- Order in the practice app, or hand the slip to your community pharmacy. The practice does not take repeat prescription requests by phone.
- Allow 3 working days before you collect. Order a week before you run out.
- Your pharmacy is the right place to ask how to take a medicine, about side effects, or about treatment for minor illnesses.

## Test results

- Blood test results usually take 5 working days. X-ray and scan results take up to 2 weeks.
- Results are shown in the practice app. To hear them by phone, call after 14:00, when the morning rush has passed.
- If a result needs action, the practice contacts you. No contact does not always mean a normal result, so check the app or call.

## Urgent care

Call 999 straight away for a life-threatening emergency, such as:

- chest pain, or pressure in the chest that spreads to the arm, neck, jaw or back
- severe difficulty breathing
- signs of a stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech
- severe bleeding that will not stop
- someone who is unconscious, or having a seizure for the first time
- a severe allergic reaction

For urgent help that is not life-threatening, call NHS 111 or use NHS 111 online, at any time of day.

For a mental health crisis, call NHS 111 and choose the mental health option. In an emergency, call 999.

The practice, its app and its virtual assistant cannot give emergency help.
