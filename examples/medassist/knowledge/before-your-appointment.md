# Harbourside Medical Centre: before your appointment

Harbourside Medical Centre is a fictional GP practice, used for training. None of these details are real.

## Blood tests

- Blood tests are taken by the practice nurse, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 12:00 only, because samples are collected by the hospital at 13:00.
- Most blood tests need no preparation, including most cholesterol tests. Eat and drink as normal.
- Fast only if the clinician who asked for the test told you to. If you are not sure whether you were told to fast, ask reception. Fasting means nothing to eat or drink except water for 12 hours before the test. Keep taking your usual medicines unless told otherwise.
- Drink plenty of water beforehand: it makes the blood easier to take.

## Vaccinations

- Travel vaccinations: book a nurse appointment at least 8 weeks before you travel, as some courses need more than one dose. Bring your travel dates and destinations.
- Children's immunisations are booked by the practice, which sends an invitation. Bring the child's red book.
- Flu and COVID-19 vaccinations run as autumn clinics; the practice contacts eligible patients.

## What to bring

- A list of the medicines you take, including ones you buy yourself.
- Your glasses or hearing aids if you use them.
- For a medication review, bring the medicines themselves or their boxes.

## Arriving

- Arrive 5 minutes early and check in at the screen in reception.
- If you arrive more than 10 minutes late, you may be asked to rebook.
