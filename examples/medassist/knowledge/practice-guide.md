# Harbourside Medical Centre: practice guide

Harbourside Medical Centre is a fictional GP practice, used for training. None of these details are real.

## Address and contact

- 4 Quay Street, Porthaven PV1 2QS
- Reception: 01632 960 123 (lines open Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:30)
- Online: book, cancel and order repeat prescriptions through the practice app

## Opening hours

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 08:00 to 18:30 |
| Saturday | 09:00 to 12:00, pre-booked appointments only; the phones are closed |
| Sunday and bank holidays | Closed |

When the practice is closed, call NHS 111 for urgent medical help that is not an emergency. In an emergency, call 999.

## Appointment types

| Type | Length | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| GP appointment | 10 minutes, face to face | New or ongoing health problems that need a doctor |
| GP phone call | 10 minutes | Follow-ups, fit notes, results that need discussing, problems that do not need an examination |
| Practice nurse | 15 minutes | Blood tests, vaccinations, wound dressings, cervical screening, blood pressure checks |
| Clinical pharmacist | 15 minutes | Medication reviews, side effects, questions about how to take a medicine |

One problem per appointment, so the clinician has time to deal with it properly. If you have several problems, book a double appointment by phone.

## Booking and cancelling

- Book up to four weeks ahead in the app or by phone. Same-day GP appointments are released at 08:00 each weekday.
- Cancel at least 24 hours before if you can, so someone else can use the slot.
- After two missed appointments in 12 months without cancelling, the practice writes to you.

## Access and support

- Step-free entrance, a lift to the first floor, and a hearing loop at reception.
- Free interpreters, including British Sign Language, for any appointment. Ask when you book; the appointment will be a double slot.
- Free parking for 12 cars, including 2 accessible bays. The 14 bus stops outside.

## Your information

The practice will only ask for the details it needs to identify you: your full name and date of birth. Staff and the practice app will never ask for your bank details.
